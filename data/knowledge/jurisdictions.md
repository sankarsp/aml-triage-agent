# Typology: High-Risk Jurisdictions and Cross-Border Exposure

## Description
Payments to or from jurisdictions subject to a FATF call for action, comprehensive sanctions, or identified as having strategic AML deficiencies carry elevated risk of sanctions evasion, proliferation financing and laundering. The institution's list of high-risk jurisdictions is maintained by Compliance and must be refreshed after each FATF plenary.

## Red flags
- Any counterparty, intermediary bank or beneficiary address in a call-for-action jurisdiction.
- Routing through third countries that appears designed to obscure the final destination.
- Customer profile with no stated link to the jurisdiction.

## Investigation steps
Screen all parties against current sanctions lists before any other analysis; a confirmed sanctions match follows the sanctions escalation procedure, not the standard alert workflow. Document the business purpose and any supporting evidence for the cross-border relationship.
