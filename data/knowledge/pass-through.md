# Typology: Pass-Through and Funnel Accounts

## Description
A pass-through account receives funds and moves them onward quickly, leaving a low balance. A funnel account collects deposits from many locations or senders and sends them to a single destination, often cross-border. These patterns are common in money mule schemes, fraud proceeds layering and trade-based laundering.

## Red flags
- Inbound wires or transfers followed within 1-3 days by outbound payments of a similar total (80% or more).
- Outbound destination in a high-risk or unrelated jurisdiction with no business rationale.
- Personal account with a salaried occupation handling business-scale flows.
- Many unrelated senders, one beneficiary.
- Descriptions that are vague ("services", "invoice", "gift") or inconsistent between legs.

## Investigation steps
Map the flow of funds: who sent, who received, time between legs, and residual balance. Check counterparties against sanctions and adverse media. Assess whether the customer may be a knowing or unwitting money mule; consider recent account changes such as new devices or contact details.
